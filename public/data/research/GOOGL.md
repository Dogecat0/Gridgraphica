## 1. Geographic Profile Summary

Alphabet Inc. operates as a preeminent global technology conglomerate, maintaining a vast and highly integrated international presence. The company's operational footprint spans over 140 countries, anchored by a sophisticated network of data centers, cloud infrastructure, and advanced AI research facilities. This strategic distribution across North America, Europe, Asia, and South America enables Alphabet to support its diverse service ecosystem and maintain a robust, scalable global workforce.

## 2. Data Center Footprint

Google maintains a robust global network of data centers, characterized by a mix of fully operational, under-construction, and planned facilities. The infrastructure is primarily owned, reflecting a strategy of vertical integration to ensure control over performance and sustainability metrics.

### 2.1 Operational Facilities

Many of the company's commissioned sites, such as those in The Dalles (OR), Dublin (Ireland), and Hamina (Finland), maintain a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of 1.1, demonstrating high energy efficiency. Key operational sites include:

*   **North America:** Bridgeport (AL), Clarksville (TN), Lithia Springs (GA), The Dalles (OR), Mesa (AZ), Moncks Corner (SC), Midlothian (TX), and several sites in South Carolina (Summerville, St. George, Ridgeville).
*   **Europe:** St. Ghislain (Belgium), Fredericia (Denmark), Hamina (Finland), Dublin (Ireland), and multiple sites in the Netherlands (Eemshaven, Middenmeer, Winschoten, Groningen).
*   **Asia-Pacific:** Changhua (Taiwan), Yunlin (Taiwan), and Singapore.
*   **South America:** Quilicura (Chile).

### 2.2 Development Pipeline

Google continues to expand its footprint with several projects currently in the construction or planning phases:

*   **Under Construction:** Pine Island (MN), Canelones (Uruguay), Armstrong County (TX), Haskell County (TX), and Kronstorf (Austria).
*   **Planned:** LaGrange (GA), Monroe County (GA), Farciennes (Belgium), Wilbarger County (TX), and Red Oak (TX).

### 2.3 Infrastructure Summary

All listed facilities are categorized as owned, ensuring consistent operational standards across the global portfolio. While PUE data is available for most commissioned sites, specific IT load and water consumption metrics remain proprietary or subject to ongoing reporting updates. The company continues to prioritize regions with access to renewable energy and favorable climatic conditions for cooling efficiency.

## 3. Energy Profile & Sustainability

### 3.1 Energy Consumption Trends
Alphabet does not report a single aggregate annual energy consumption figure in MWh. However, the company has acknowledged significant scaling in its infrastructure requirements, with global data center electricity consumption projected to double between 2022 and 2026, driven primarily by the surge in AI compute demand.

### 3.2 Energy Mix and Renewables
Alphabet continues to advance its transition toward a 24/7 carbon-free energy (CFE) model. As of 2024, the company achieved 66% CFE on an hourly basis. This progress is underpinned by a robust procurement strategy, totaling over 22 GW of cumulative clean energy capacity since 2010, spanning wind, solar, geothermal, and nuclear sources.

### 3.3 Traditional Energy and Emissions
Despite aggressive clean energy procurement, the company remains reliant on grid-supplied electricity that includes fossil fuel components. This dependency is reflected in the 11% year-on-year increase in total emissions reported in 2025, highlighting the challenges of balancing rapid infrastructure growth with decarbonization targets.

### 3.4 Strategic Sustainability Initiatives
Alphabet has implemented several high-impact initiatives to reach its goal of 24/7 CFE by 2030:
* **Nuclear Energy:** A landmark agreement for 500 MW of small modular reactor (SMR) capacity with Kairos Power.
* **Energy Storage & Grid Integration:** A 1.9 GW clean energy partnership in Minnesota incorporating iron-air battery storage technology.
* **Geothermal Innovation:** Continued development of advanced geothermal projects in collaboration with Fervo Energy.
* **Supply Chain Mandates:** A requirement for major hardware suppliers to match 100% of their electricity consumption with clean energy sources by 2029.

*Sources: Alphabet 10th Annual Environmental Report (June 2025); Alphabet 2026 Infrastructure and PPA Announcements; Alphabet 2024 Clean Energy Procurement Data.*
